# Position Changes Everything

Monuments. We see them everywhere. The Acropolis. Stonehenge. Mount Rushmore. Machu Picchu. Big Ben. The Statue of Liberty. Statues in the Capitol Rotunda. Small town memorials for veterans. We call them monuments for a reason. They are the products of ages and nations. They pay tribute to something that made a mark upon the world, whether great or evil. We look on these monuments today and learn about the people who built them.

## Called to Be Monuments

Like these monuments, God wants us, the church, to be a monument too. Through us, He can show the world something of Himself that they will never see elsewhere. He can do something in the weak, tenuous, feeble, followers of His that makes them strong. He can cause His glory to shine through them in such a way that the world can't help but stop and say, "Did you see that?!"

## The Power of Remembering

Throughout this issue, you will find the theme of reflecting. Reflecting or remembering is important. It makes us pause and evaluate where we are going. Again and again in the Bible, we are told to remember. In fact, the word 'remember' appears over 1200 times in the Bible. However, God doesn't want us to just relive memories and talk about past events for fun. Those spiritual monuments of the past serve as a forward call. They urge us to turn toward God with a renewed zeal. It is almost ironic. The monuments of the past open the door of opportunity for us to become monuments ourselves.

That is why this newspaper is called Monumental Times. We want to remember the spiritual monuments of the past with the intent to build the current era of the church into a monumental time in history. It starts with us looking back, but it doesn't end there. Remembering takes time and some effort, but what does it mean to be a monumental era of history?

## Understanding Our Position

I believe it all starts with understanding (or remembering) who we are, who God is, and what our position is. We are sinners who rebelled against God. God is a perfect and holy God who is not anything like us. He came and died for our sin so that we wouldn't have to – but He took it further. He gave us His perfect righteousness and spotless reputation and took our sin-scarred one. Then, He came back to life so that we can have His power in us to win the victory over sin.

When we accept His death on our behalf, we enter into Him, and we go where He goes. Our sin nature died with Him, and we rise to new life in Him. The journey doesn't end there. He ascended to heaven and sits at the right hand of the Father. If we are in Him, we sit there with Him too. Ephesians tells us exactly where Christ went:

> "The working of His mighty power which He worked in Christ when He raised Him from the dead and seated Him at His right hand in the heavenly places, far above all principality and power and might and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this age but also in that which is to come. And He put all things under His feet, and gave Him to be head over all things to the church, which is His body, the fullness of Him who fills all in all."*Ephesians 1:19-23*

## We Are There Too

Ephesians then tells us we are there too because we are in Christ:

> "But God, who is rich in mercy, because of His great love with which He loved us, even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved), and raised us up together, and made us sit together in the heavenly places in Christ Jesus, that in the ages to come He might show the exceeding riches of His grace in His kindness toward us in Christ Jesus."*Ephesians 2:4-7*

This is where you are if you are in Christ! You are seated over all authorities and powers because you are IN CHRIST! Be reminded, it is not any of your doing and you do not deserve to be there, but the facts remain. You are there in Christ. The power of sin in your life is gone. Why do you fear? You, in Christ, are over all things. He has all authority. We act like we are drowning when we are already in the boat! Why? Do not let sin rule! You are IN CHRIST.

## Living in Two Realms

Christ's physical man is in heaven, but His spirit is in you. Your physical man is here, but your spiritual man is in Christ, in heaven. You are in heaven, seated over all things, now. Think about it. You are there this moment. It is not a someday or sometime. It is now. Let this truth sink into your hearts. Let it change your life.

## A Monumental Generation

When a generation of the church grasps this – the power of their position in Christ – the world will be changed. That generation will become a spiritual monument for that age and ages to come. God is looking for Christians who understand the gospel and live it out. He can accomplish His purposes for His glory through them, because they will be bravehearted and bending to His will.

> "Now to Him who is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that works in us, to Him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus to all generations, forever and ever. Amen."*Ephesians 3:20-21*
