# On Prayer

Prayer is by far one of the most crucial aspects of the Christian life. How are we to know God, be transformed to be like Christ, or battle for Him, if we never talk to Him? Indeed, half the battle is won when we put our lives into His hands instead of holding on to it ourselves. We should know by now that it is foolish to hold onto control!

I have found praying Psalm 139:23-24 to be a great place to start as I humble myself and turn to God. As you pray in the next weeks, try praying this scripture and allow God to convict you of sin and restore you to truth.

> "Search me, O God, and know my heart; try me, and know my anxieties; and see if there is any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting."*Psalm 139:23-24*
