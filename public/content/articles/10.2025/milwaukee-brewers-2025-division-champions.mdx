# Win for WI: Milwaukee Brewers Win Central Division

The Milwaukee Brewers won the Central Division in the 2025 season, finishing 5 games ahead of their rival, the Chicago Cubs. The team broke several records, including a franchise win streak with 14 wins in August.

## Record-Breaking Performance

Their exceptional base running contributed extensively to the historic season. The Brewers demonstrated outstanding teamwork and athletic prowess throughout the year, culminating in one of the best seasons in franchise history.

## A Tribute to Bob Ueker

For many fans and players alike, the phenomenal season serves as a tribute to the legacy of the late Bob Ueker, who passed away in January 2025. Ueker's decades-long association with the Brewers as the beloved voice of the team left an indelible mark on Wisconsin baseball culture.

The 2025 championship run reminded fans across Wisconsin why they love America's pastime and why the Brewers hold a special place in the hearts of Badger State residents.
