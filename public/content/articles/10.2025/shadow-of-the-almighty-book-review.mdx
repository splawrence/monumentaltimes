# Shadow of the Almighty
## The Life & Testament of Jim Elliot
**By Elisabeth Elliot**

Jim Elliot was young, passionate, and on fire for the Lord, taking advantage of every opportunity for the glory of God. Through life's ups and downs, he steadfastly dedicated his 28 years to the work of the Lord before paying the ultimate price for his sacrifice.

## A Legacy in Words

Elisabeth Elliot, Jim Elliot's wife, masterfully compiled the letters and journal entries from Jim Elliot after his death in 1956, compiling the thoughts, struggles, breakthroughs, and passions of a young man with a driving purpose. When I first picked up this book, I wasn't sure whether to expect the same old run-of-the-mill story I'd heard so many times before. But reading through Jim Elliot's writings impacted me more than I imagined.

## Lessons from a Life Well-Lived

Jim Elliot wrote especially throughout his years going to school and while preparing for the missionary journey to Ecuador that would eventually take his life. Each lesson he learned in trusting the Lord and leaning on his Savior in every situation was a lesson for me as well, and I was refreshed and renewed in my desire to know the Lord more and keep pressing into my faith.

## My Recommendation

I highly recommend reading this book, or listening to it read by Elisabeth Elliot herself. If you allow your heart and mind to be open to the Lord's work, there is no telling how *Shadow of the Almighty* could minister to you as well.

**Recommended for ages 16 and up.**
