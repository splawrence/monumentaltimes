# S'mores Bars

*From Cook's Country Magazine*\
*From the Kitchen of Pam Hanson*

## Ingredients

* Graham Crackers
* 5 tablespoons butter (unsalted)
* ¼ cup brown sugar
* ½ teaspoon salt
* ½ cup milk chocolate chips
* Mini marshmallows

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. Line an 8x8 square pan with graham crackers. Make them fit carefully and break off any graham crackers that don't fit if needed.
3. For the caramel mixture, heat the butter, brown sugar, and salt together in a pan until boiling. Pour the mixture over the graham crackers when finished.
4. Spread chocolate chips over the top of the caramel mixture.
5. Bake in preheated oven for 10 minutes until it bubbles.
6. Take out of oven and sprinkle top with mini marshmallows.
7. Cool in freezer. Once cooled, break into pieces and serve.

***

*Enjoy this campfire classic in bar form!*
