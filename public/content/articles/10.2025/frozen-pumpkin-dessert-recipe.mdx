# Frozen Pumpkin Dessert

*From Aunt Pam*\
*From the Kitchen of Pam Hanson*

## Ingredients

**Crust:**

* 2 cups ginger snap cookies, crumbled
* 6 tablespoons butter

**Layers:**

* Vanilla ice cream
* 2 cups pumpkin
* 1 cup sugar
* ½ cup brown sugar
* ½ teaspoon nutmeg
* ¼ teaspoon cloves
* 1 teaspoon cinnamon
* 8 ounces cool whip

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Prepare crust by putting crumbled ginger snaps in a 9x13 pan. Melt the butter, pour over the top, and combine cookies and butter well. Bake in preheated oven for 8 minutes. Set aside to cool.
3. Whip together in a medium sized bowl the pumpkin, sugar, brown sugar, nutmeg, cloves, and cinnamon.
4. Spread a 1 inch layer of vanilla ice cream on top of the cooled crust.
5. Spread finished pumpkin layer over the top of the ice cream.
6. Spread cool whip over the top of the pumpkin for the final layer.
7. Freeze the whole pan. When ready to serve, cut into squares and display your layers on a platter.
